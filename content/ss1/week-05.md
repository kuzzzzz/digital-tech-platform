# WEEK 5 — MIDTERM TEST

**Coverage:** Weeks 1–4 — Introduction to Digital Technologies, History & Evolution, Number Systems, Computer Hardware
**Duration:** 1 hour 15 minutes (both periods)
**Total:** 40 marks

## Instructions to the Teacher
Use Period 1 (45 minutes) for the test proper and Period 2 (45 minutes) to mark on the spot with the class where possible, or to review the answers. Immediate feedback after a test is worth more than the test itself.

### SECTION A — Objective Questions (15 marks)
*Answer all questions. Each carries 1 mark.*

1. Digital technology stores information as ______. (a) waves (b) 0s and 1s (c) colours (d) letters
2. Which device is analogue? (a) Digital camera (b) Vinyl record player (c) Flash drive (d) Smartphone
3. The father of the computer is ______. (a) Pascal (b) Babbage (c) Turing (d) Hollerith
4. First-generation computers used ______. (a) transistors (b) ICs (c) vacuum tubes (d) microprocessors
5. Ada Lovelace is remembered as the first ______. (a) engineer (b) programmer (c) mathematician (d) inventor
6. 11001₂ in decimal is ______. (a) 21 (b) 25 (c) 27 (d) 31
7. The hexadecimal digit E equals ______ in decimal. (a) 12 (b) 13 (c) 14 (d) 15
8. To convert binary to octal, group the digits in ______. (a) twos (b) threes (c) fours (d) fives
9. 45₁₀ in binary is ______. (a) 101101 (b) 101011 (c) 110101 (d) 100101
10. Which unit performs calculations in the CPU? (a) CU (b) ALU (c) RAM (d) ROM
11. RAM is described as ______. (a) permanent (b) volatile (c) optical (d) magnetic
12. MICR is mainly used to read ______. (a) barcodes (b) fingerprints (c) cheques (d) shaded marks
13. Which of these is **not** inside the system unit? (a) Motherboard (b) PSU (c) Scanner (d) RAM
14. One gigabyte is approximately ______ megabytes. (a) 8 (b) 100 (c) 1,024 (d) 10,000
15. GSM services were launched in Nigeria in ______. (a) 1992 (b) 1999 (c) 2001 (d) 2007

### SECTION B — Theory (25 marks)
*Answer any THREE questions.*

**1.** (a) Define digital technology. **(2 marks)**
 (b) State four differences between analogue and digital technology. **(4 marks)**
 (c) Mention three challenges facing digital technology adoption in Nigeria. **(3 marks)**

**2.** (a) Complete the table for the five generations of computers (technology and one example each). **(5 marks)**
 (b) State the contribution of Charles Babbage and Herman Hollerith to computing. **(4 marks)**

**3.** (a) Convert 156₁₀ to (i) binary (ii) octal (iii) hexadecimal. **(6 marks)**
 (b) Convert 1AF₁₆ to decimal. **(3 marks)**

**4.** (a) Draw and label the block diagram of a computer system. **(5 marks)**
 (b) State four differences between RAM and ROM. **(4 marks)**

**5.** (a) Differentiate between impact and non-impact printers, giving one example of each. **(4 marks)**
 (b) Classify these as input or output: OMR, plotter, microphone, projector, barcode reader. **(5 marks)**

**Selected worked answers:**
- Q3(a): 156₁₀ = **10011100₂** = **234₈** = **9C₁₆**
- Q3(b): 1AF₁₆ = (1×256) + (10×16) + 15 = 256 + 160 + 15 = **431₁₀**

## TEACHER'S HELPER NOTES — WEEK 5
- **Record the marks properly.** In the Nigerian CA system this score usually carries 15 of the 40 CA marks. Enter it into the continuous assessment record book the same week.
- **Analyse the failures, don't just count them.** Note which question most students got wrong. If it is the conversions (it usually is), you now know to spend ten minutes of every subsequent lesson on a conversion drill until Week 10.
- **Mark in front of them if class size allows.** Swap scripts, work through the objectives on the board, let students mark a neighbour's paper, then collect for the theory section. This turns a test into a teaching hour.
- **Watch for copying** in a crowded classroom by preparing two versions with the objective options shuffled.
- **Do not give the test as homework.** The whole value is in seeing what students can do unaided.
